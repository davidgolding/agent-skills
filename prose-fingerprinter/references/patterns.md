# Prose Fingerprinter Patterns & Anti-Patterns

This document defines the patterns and anti-patterns used by prose-fingerprinter.

## Patterns

- **Name**: Multi-Dimensional Stylistic Audit
- **When**: While building a prose fingerprint for any input passage. Synthesize syntactic architecture ratios, prosodic scansion profiles, etymological registries, presentation-mode mixes, and thematic clusters into a unified consciousness-engine description rather than presenting isolated metrics.
- **Example**: ```
  Identify that the combination of high parataxis (flat landscape), dominant
  spondaic cadences (rhythmic brakes), high Germanic/Anglo-Saxon vocabulary
  affinity (sensory grounding), and an Action/Description presentation mix
  creates a stoic, unmediated consciousness.
  ```

---

- **Name**: Fingerprint Report Shape
- **When**: While laying out the fingerprint for a single passage.
- **Example**: ```
  Report the five dimensions in fixed order — syntactic architecture,
  prosodic scansion, etymological register, presentation-mode mix, thematic
  clustering — giving each one its measurement, the basis of that measurement
  (counted exhaustively, or estimated from a stated sample), and the stylistic
  function the measurement performs. Close with the consciousness-engine
  synthesis, which reads the five dimensions together as one worldview.
  ```

---

- **Name**: Comparison Table Construction
- **When**: While fingerprinting two or more texts in one invocation.
- **Example**: ```
  | Dimension            | Text A                  | Text B                    |
  |----------------------|-------------------------|---------------------------|
  | Syntactic            | 78% parataxis (counted) | 31% parataxis (counted)   |
  | Prosodic             | spondaic brakes         | iambic forward pull       |
  | Etymological         | Germanic-dominant       | Latinate-dominant         |
  | Presentation mix     | Action / Description    | Summary / Interior        |
  | Thematic             | endurance, weather      | obligation, institutions  |
  | Consciousness engine | stoic, unmediated       | deliberative, mediated    |

  One row per dimension, one column per text, the synthesis as the closing row.
  ```

---

- **Name**: Representative Sampling
- **When**: While fingerprinting a passage long enough to strain a single close reading — a chapter, a book, anything past roughly 3,000 words.
- **Example**: ```
  Select 1,000 to 2,000 words spanning the text's range rather than its
  opening alone, and disclose the sample's size and location alongside the
  figures drawn from it. For a longer work, fingerprint several chunks and
  average the dimensions across them, saying how many chunks the average
  rests on.
  ```

---

## Anti-Patterns

- **Name**: Flat Metric Reporting
- **Why**: Presenting raw statistics — "30% parataxis, 12 spondees, 40% Action" — without translating them into their stylistic purpose leaves the fingerprint abstract and dry. The music and the consciousness of a text surface only once each number is paired with the effect it produces.
- **Instead**: Pair every quantitative finding with a qualitative explanation of *how* that mechanic functions — for example, "The high coordinate ratio removes the authorial guiding hand, forcing the reader to encounter sensory details directly."

---

- **Name**: Undisclosed Estimation
- **Why**: A figure produced by inspection and reported in the register of an exhaustive count invites a precision the analysis has not earned, and it leaves the reader unable to tell which parts of the fingerprint would survive a recount.
- **Instead**: Measurement Basis Disclosure — state for each figure whether it was counted across the whole passage or estimated from a sample, naming the sample when one was used.

---

- **Name**: Single-Author Projection
- **Why**: Reaching for a famous exemplar — declaring a passage "Hemingway" or "Faulknerian" — substitutes a known signature for the one being extracted, and the exemplar's other traits then bleed into the reading of a passage that shows no sign of them.
- **Instead**: Describe the passage's own mechanics first and completely; introduce a comparison only after the fingerprint stands on its own, and frame it as a resemblance on named dimensions rather than an identification.
