# Prose Fingerprinter Patterns & Anti-Patterns

This document defines the patterns and anti-patterns used by prose-fingerprinter.

## Patterns

- **Name**: Multi-Dimensional Stylistic Audit
- **Description**: Synthesize syntactic architecture ratios, prosodic scansion profiles, etymological registries, presentation mode mixes, and thematic clusters into a unified "consciousness engine" description rather than presenting isolated metrics.
- **When**: Building a prose fingerprint for any input passage.
- **Example**:
```
Identify that the combination of high parataxis (flat landscape), dominant spondaic cadences (rhythmic brakes), high Germanic/Anglo-Saxon vocabulary affinity (sensory grounding), and an Action/Description presentation mix creates a stoic, unmediated consciousness (e.g., Hemingway).
```

---

## Anti-Patterns

- **Name**: Flat Metric Reporting
- **Description**: Presenting raw statistics (e.g., "30% parataxis, 12 spondees, 40% Action") without translating them into their stylistic purpose or explanation of how they manipulate the reader's attention.
- **Why**: Raw numbers alone do not reveal the "music" or the "consciousness" of the text, leaving the fingerprint abstract and dry.
- **Instead**: Pair every quantitative finding with a qualitative stylistic explanation of *how* that mechanic functions (e.g., "The high coordinate ratio removes the authorial guiding hand, forcing the reader to encounter sensory details directly").
