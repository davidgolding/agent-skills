# APA (7th ed.)

Condensed guidance for mechanical/copy/reference passes; the manual governs.

- **Serial comma:** yes, required before "and"/"or" in a series (APA 7, ch. 6).
- **Quotation marks:** double for first-level, single for nested. Use for direct quotations under 40 words; block-quote (indented, no quotation marks) at 40+ words.
- **Dashes:** em dash for breaks in thought, no surrounding spaces.
- **Numbers:** use numerals for 10 and above, and for numbers representing statistics, exact measurements, or anything preceding a unit of measurement; spell out below 10 in most other cases (APA 7, ch. 6).
- **Dates/time:** month day, year in prose; APA reference-list dates follow the source (year, or year, month day).
- **Capitalization:** sentence-case for article/chapter titles in reference-list entries; title-case in-text; sentence-case for headings.
- **Abbreviations:** define on first use in text; used freely thereafter if it aids concision.
- **Italics:** titles of standalone works (books, reports) in the reference list; statistical symbols (e.g., *p*, *M*, *SD*); not used for mere emphasis.
- **Citation architecture:** in-text parenthetical or narrative author-date, e.g. `(Author, Year)` or "Author (Year)"; page/paragraph number added for direct quotations. Full reference-list entry, alphabetized by author surname, hanging indent. Locator format: a section number (e.g. `APA 7, section 8.x`) only when confirmed present in the loaded manual, otherwise `(APA 7, reference list)`.
