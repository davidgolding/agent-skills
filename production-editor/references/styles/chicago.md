# Chicago (CMOS 18 / Turabian 9)

Condensed guidance for mechanical/copy/reference passes. This is not a reproduction of the manual--it covers the categories those passes actually need. The manual governs; when citing a rule not listed here, cite by chapter or topic name rather than inventing a section number (see `references/citation-protocol.md`).

- **Serial comma:** yes, always (CMOS 18, ch. 6).
- **Quotation marks:** double for first-level quotes, single for quotes-within-quotes. Commas and periods go inside closing quotation marks; colons and semicolons go outside (CMOS 18, ch. 13).
- **Dashes:** em dash, no surrounding spaces, for breaks in thought or parenthetical asides. En dash for number ranges ("pp. 3-10") and some compound adjectives. Hyphen for compounds (CMOS 18, ch. 6).
- **Numbers:** spell out zero through one hundred and round multiples thereof; use numerals for 101 and above, and for exact measurements, percentages, and dates (CMOS 18, ch. 9).
- **Dates/time:** month day, year ("March 5, 2026"), no ordinals. Time as "10:00 a.m." with lowercase periods.
- **Capitalization:** headline-style ("title case") for titles of works by default; sentence-style available as an alternate house choice--record which is in force on the style sheet.
- **Abbreviations/acronyms:** spell out on first use with the abbreviation in parentheses; abbreviate thereafter. Keep acronyms period-free (US, not U.S., is a house-style choice--record on style sheet).
- **Italics:** titles of standalone works (books, journals, films); foreign words not yet naturalized (on first use only); emphasis (sparingly).
- **Citation architecture:**
  - *Notes-bibliography:* full note on first citation, shortened note thereafter, full bibliography entry alphabetized by author surname. Locator format: `(CMOS 18, note n)` or `(CMOS 18, bibliography)`.
  - *Author-date:* parenthetical `(Author Year, page)`, full reference-list entry alphabetized by author surname. Locator format: `(CMOS 18, reference list)`.
  - Record which architecture is in force for the project on the style sheet rather than assuming it.
